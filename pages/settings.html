❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
How does the Data Ingestion & Conflict Resolver work?

When you upload a new CHARUSAT semester scorecard or transcript (JSON/CSV), the ingestion engine parses records, checks for differences against your active database, and shows a side-by-side diff. You can selectively choose whether to accept new values or preserve existing verified data.

How do I calculate minimum attendance eligibility?

CHARUSAT academic regulations require a minimum of 75% aggregate attendance in each registered theory and laboratory course. Use the Attendance Calculator tab to track shortage recovery and safe bunk limits.

Where can I download my official semester fee receipt?

Navigate to Payments & Receipts or College Fees. Click "View Receipt" next to any completed transaction (e.g. ₹64,000 Sem 3 installment) to view and print your digitally sealed receipt.
